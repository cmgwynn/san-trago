08 / Save failure / Honest recovery

Never call unsaved progress saved.

Progress not saved

We couldn't save your progress.

Your current session is still here. The last good checkpoint has not been replaced. Show an actionable reason when available, such as a storage or permission problem.

Retry saveShow details

Keep this checkpoint transition pending until saving succeeds. Do not begin a new shift or replay settlement when Retry is selected.

Need to leave anyway?

Quitting may lose changes since the last successful save. Show the checkpoint's day and phase when available, then require explicit confirmation.

Stay hereQuit without saving

Variants: failed purchase persistence shows the purchased item as pending save, not safely stored. Missing opening-checkpoint persistence prevents service from starting. Corrupt or unsupported saves get a separate recovery prompt; never overwrite them with a new game without consent.

Review / Before interaction experiments

What we need to learn from these screens

  • Can both creators locate every action and explain its state change?
  • Are orders, glass availability, and the current drink readable at a glance?
  • Does the service view feel like a bar, rather than a dashboard? The Godot experiment should test spatial props in place of some panels.
  • Is wrong-order recovery forgiving enough without hiding real mistakes?
  • Does washing look like a useful rhythm rather than a chore to escape?
  • Can players understand purchases, costs, checkpoint limits, and save failures?
  • Check primary 1280 x 800 and smaller 1024 x 768 desktop windows. Browser reflow is only a layout reference; Godot must be tested separately.
  • Approve or revise information placement before comparing discrete and light tactile preparation. No final art or game implementation is approved by this storyboard.

Return feedback by screen number: what is confusing, what is missing, and what should feel different. The second creator's review and complete-day approval remain outstanding.
